Support

PASS LOOP for iPhone · iOS 17 or later

Questions, bugs or ideas: write to babayaga@babayagaapps.com. Please mention your iPhone model, iOS version and the PASS LOOP version (Settings → Version). We usually reply within a few days.

Bring your Anki decks

  1. In Anki on your computer, export the deck as an Anki Deck Package (.apkg). Turn on scheduling information so your progress comes along.
  2. Get the file onto your iPhone — save it to the Files app or iCloud Drive, or AirDrop it.
  3. In PASS LOOP, go to Decks → ··· (Deck Tools) → Import Anki File, choose the file and pick the decks you want.

Got an .apkg file in Mail, Messages or Safari? Tap it, choose Share → PASS LOOP, and the import opens right away.

The import keeps going even if you close the screen; if iOS stops it, it resumes the next time you open the app. At least 30,000 cards from the decks you pick can be imported at a time (up to 150,000, depending on your iPhone's memory), and full collection files (.colpkg) work too. If some part of a card can't be shown, the import screen lists the reasons and how many cards each one affects.

Make cards with the AI you already use

In the Decks tab, start making cards with AI, pick a topic, how many cards and what kind, and choose your AI. PASS LOOP copies a ready-made request and opens that AI. Paste it there (add your own notes if you like), copy the answer, come back and paste — it becomes a deck. PASS LOOP never calls an AI itself. You can also type cards in or import a CSV file.

No Anki? Ask an AI for an .apkg file

For more than 50 cards, open Import Anki File → Ask an AI to make an .apkg, enter a topic and a card count, and PASS LOOP copies a request for an AI app that can run code. Attach your own photos, PDFs, audio or video if you like. Open the .apkg file the AI gives you and choose Share → PASS LOOP. These are front-and-back cards.

Export, back up, move to a new iPhone

  • Export a deck: choose Export on the deck, then Anki Deck (.apkg) with Include Study Progress to open it in Anki, or CSV (Spreadsheet).
  • Backups: Settings → Study Data → Backup & Restore → Create Backup File. The file holds your card progress, decks, deck options, notes and Looplet House.
  • Auto Backups: once a day your study data is saved on the iPhone and the latest 7 are kept. Images and audio aren't included, so make a backup file for a full copy.

Moving to a new iPhone

  1. On the old iPhone, create a backup file and include images and audio.
  2. Save it to the Files app or iCloud Drive, or AirDrop it to the new iPhone.
  3. On the new iPhone, open PASS LOOP → Settings → Study Data → Backup & Restore → Restore from Backup File. The restore is applied the next time the app opens.

PASS LOOP Ultra

  • Ultra is a one-time purchase with Listen Mode, the Ultra Collection for Looplet House and 9 app icon colors. Everything that is free stays free.
  • 7-day free trial: no automatic billing. When it ends only Ultra features lock — your decks, study history and coins stay as they are.
  • Family Sharing is on, so your family group gets Ultra too.
  • Restore purchases: Settings → Support PASS LOOP → Restore Purchases.
  • Refunds go through Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com. A refund turns Ultra features off but never deletes your decks, history or coins.
  • Tips in Support PASS LOOP are an optional thank-you and don't unlock any features.

Listen Mode

Start it from the headphones button on the study screen. With earphones: press once to pause, twice for Okay, three times for Again. Hard and Easy, Listen Again and Undo are on the Listen screen. Cards are read with your iPhone's own voices, or with an optional natural AI voice that runs on your iPhone (a one-time download of about 400 MB; nothing is sent to a server). The AI voice needs an iPhone with 4 GB of memory or more, and it doesn't read Chinese; other iPhones and Chinese cards use the iPhone's voices. For a nicer iPhone voice, download one for free in iOS Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices.

Good to know

  • iPhone only, iOS 17 or later — no iPad, Android or web version.
  • The app is in English, Korean, Japanese and Traditional Chinese, following your iPhone's language.
  • Home and Lock Screen widgets show today's cards, your streak and Looplet's weather. Long-press the Home Screen and add PASS LOOP.
  • No sync and no account. Move decks with .apkg files, and your progress and Looplet House with a backup file.
  • Anki add-ons don't run in PASS LOOP.
  • Everything is stored on your iPhone. See the Privacy Policy.

Report a problem

Tell us what happened, the steps that lead to it, and your iOS and PASS LOOP versions; a screenshot helps a lot. If one deck causes trouble, describe it — only attach the deck file if you're happy for us to see its contents.
