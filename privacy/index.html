Privacy Policy

Effective November 14, 2026 · bitglass

bitglass ("we") makes the iPhone and Android app PASS LOOP and runs the website babayagaapps.com. This policy explains what information they handle. It is also published to meet Article 30 of Korea's Personal Information Protection Act.

  • PASS LOOP has no account, no server of ours, no ads and no analytics or tracking. The optional AI voice is downloaded once from Hugging Face or GitHub and then runs on your phone.
  • Your decks, cards and study history stay on your phone. They are never sent to us.
  • Features that use platform services are handled by them: weather, city search and purchases by Apple on iPhone, and by Google and MET Norway on Android. The app never reads your device location (GPS).
  • The website counts visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics, without cookies and without identifying you. Otherwise only the hosting provider's server logs exist.

The app — AI voice download (optional, Ultra)

If you choose to download the AI voice in Listen Mode, the app downloads the Supertonic 3 voice model (about 400 MB) made by Supertone Inc. from Hugging Face (huggingface.co), or from our unmodified copy on GitHub (github.com) when Hugging Face is unavailable. Like any download, the request carries your IP address and basic device and app information, handled under each provider's privacy policy (Hugging Face, GitHub). No study content or account information is sent. The voice is then made on your phone, and the text it reads never leaves the device. Nothing is downloaded unless you tap the download button. On Android the request also names the app and version in its header (for example "PassLoop-Android/1.3.1").

The app — microphone, camera and motion (optional)

These permissions are asked only the first time you use the feature, and the rest of the app works if you say no. Everything below is used on your phone only. It is never sent to us or anywhere else.

  • Microphone: used when you record your pronunciation on the study screen to compare it with the card audio, or when you attach a recording to a card. A recording made on the study screen is a temporary file, deleted when you move to another card, close the screen or start Listen. A recording you attach is stored on your phone as that card's attachment. The app does not analyze recordings or use them for speech recognition. A recording lasts up to 2 minutes.
  • Camera (iPhone): used only when you take a photo to attach to a card. The photo is stored on your phone as that card's attachment. On Android the camera app takes the photo and PASS LOOP asks for no camera permission.
  • Motion (iPhone, optional): only if you turn on "Rate by head movement" in Settings > Listen > Experimental, the app reads the head direction values from AirPods that track head movement, to detect a nod or a shake. The values are used on the spot, never saved, and never leave your phone. Nothing is read while it is off. This does not exist on Android.
  • Speech recognition: the app does no speech recognition. When you say "Start Listen" to Siri or use Shortcuts on iPhone, Apple handles the speech under Apple's privacy policy, and PASS LOOP receives only the request to start Listen, never your voice or the words. Android has no Siri connection; you start Listen by long-pressing the app icon.
  • Lock Screen: during Listen, the Lock Screen (Live Activity and Dynamic Island on iPhone, a notification on Android) shows the deck name, the number of cards left and the Again, Pause and Okay buttons. Card text is not shown. It updates inside the app, with no push server.
  • Photos and recordings you attach follow the same rules as the card: they can be included in exports and backup files, and in your phone's backup (iCloud on iPhone, Google on Android).

The app — when you send something yourself

  • Making cards with an outside AI: the app never calls an AI. It copies a request to your clipboard and opens the AI app or site you choose; whatever you paste there is handled under that service's policy.
  • Sharing, exporting and backup files: .apkg, CSV and backup files (.passloopbackup) go wherever you choose (Files, iCloud Drive, AirDrop and so on). Backup files are not encrypted, so keep them somewhere safe.
  • Links: links inside cards open in your browser when you tap them.
  • Writing to us from the app (iPhone and Android): Settings > About > "Send a Question, Bug or Idea" opens your own mail app with a drafted message: a subject, a short prompt, and one line with the app version, system version, phone model and language, which you can edit or delete. PASS LOOP itself sends nothing; the email goes from your mail account to us and is handled like any email you write to us.

2. How long we keep it, and deletion

  • App data stays on your phone until you delete it or delete the app, which removes the app's storage. Deleted decks can be restored from the trash or deleted permanently.
  • Pronunciation recordings made on the study screen are temporary and deleted when you move on. Photos and recordings you attach stay on your phone until you delete the card or remove the attachment.
  • Automatic backups: at most once a day the app backs up your study data on the device and keeps the latest 7 (images and audio excluded). When you restore from a backup, your previous data is kept on the device as a safety copy (the latest 3). All of these live in the app's storage and are removed with the app.
  • Android device backup: depending on your Android settings, the app's data (decks, study history and settings) can be included in Google's backup of your phone to your Google account, which Google controls under its own terms. The AI voice model and the widget copies are not included.
  • Copies outside the app — phone backups (iCloud on iPhone, Google on Android), files you export, text you paste into an AI — are under your control.
  • Emails are kept for one year after we reply, then deleted without delay.
  • Thank-you nicknames stay in the public list until you ask us to remove them; we then take them down without delay.
  • Website logs and visit statistics follow each provider's retention policy.

5. Your rights

Everything the app stores is on your phone, so you can view, change and delete it in the app. For anything related to your emails, you can ask us to show, correct, delete or stop processing it at babayaga@babayagaapps.com, and we'll act without delay.

6. How we protect information

  • App data is protected by the iOS app sandbox and device encryption and is never sent to a server of ours.
  • On Android, app data is protected by the app sandbox and your phone's encryption and is never sent to a server of ours.
  • The screens that show cards block outside network access.
  • Backup files are checked file by file with hashes before they are restored.
  • Support email is kept in an account only we can access.

7. Cookies and trackers

The app has no cookies, advertising identifiers or tracking tools. The website sets no cookies; its sound and day/night settings, the opening-seen mark and the game's records stay in your browser's storage. Cloudflare Web Analytics, which counts visits, stores nothing on your device.

8. Children

PASS LOOP has no account and we receive no personal information, so we don't ask for age. If a child under 14 uses outside AI services or links, a parent or guardian should check them together.
