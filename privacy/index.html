Privacy Policy

Effective October 18, 2026 · bitglass

bitglass ("we") makes the iPhone app PASS LOOP and runs the website babayagaapps.com. This policy explains what information they handle. It is also published to meet Article 30 of Korea's Personal Information Protection Act.

  • PASS LOOP has no account, no server of ours, no ads and no analytics or tracking. The optional AI voice is downloaded once from Hugging Face or GitHub and then runs on your iPhone.
  • Your decks, cards and study history stay on your iPhone. They are never sent to us.
  • Features that use Apple services — weather, city search and purchases — are handled by Apple. The app never reads your device location (GPS).
  • The website collects no visitor statistics. Only the hosting provider's server logs exist, and it sets no cookies.

Apple handles this information under its own privacy policy (apple.com/legal/privacy). The app does not ask for location permission and never reads GPS location. Without a weather city, no weather request is made. Study reminders are local notifications scheduled on your iPhone; there is no push server. Reading aloud uses your iPhone's voices and sends nothing.

The app — AI voice download (optional, Ultra)

If you choose to download the AI voice in Listen Mode, the app downloads the Supertonic 3 voice model (about 400 MB) made by Supertone Inc. from Hugging Face (huggingface.co), or from our unmodified copy on GitHub (github.com) when Hugging Face is unavailable. Like any download, the request carries your IP address and basic device and app information, handled under each provider's privacy policy (Hugging Face, GitHub). No study content or account information is sent. The voice is then made on your iPhone, and the text it reads never leaves the device. Nothing is downloaded unless you tap the download button.

The app — when you send something yourself

  • Making cards with an outside AI: the app never calls an AI. It copies a request to your clipboard and opens the AI app or site you choose; whatever you paste there is handled under that service's policy.
  • Sharing, exporting and backup files: .apkg, CSV and backup files (.passloopbackup) go wherever you choose (Files, iCloud Drive, AirDrop and so on). Backup files are not encrypted, so keep them somewhere safe.
  • Links: links inside cards open in your browser when you tap them.

2. How long we keep it, and deletion

  • App data stays on your iPhone until you delete it or delete the app, which removes the app's storage. Deleted decks can be restored from the trash or deleted permanently.
  • Automatic backups: at most once a day the app backs up your study data on the device and keeps the latest 7 (images and audio excluded). When you restore from a backup, your previous data is kept on the device as a safety copy (the latest 3). All of these live in the app's storage and are removed with the app.
  • Copies outside the app — iPhone/iCloud device backups, files you export, text you paste into an AI — are under your control.
  • Emails are kept for one year after we reply, then deleted without delay.
  • Thank-you nicknames stay in the public list until you ask us to remove them; we then take them down without delay.
  • Website logs follow the hosting providers' retention policies.

3. Sharing with third parties

We do not share personal information with third parties. We never receive your app data.

5. Your rights

Everything the app stores is on your iPhone, so you can view, change and delete it in the app. For anything related to your emails, you can ask us to show, correct, delete or stop processing it at babayaga@babayagaapps.com, and we'll act without delay.

6. How we protect information

  • App data is protected by the iOS app sandbox and device encryption and is never sent to a server of ours.
  • The screens that show cards block outside network access.
  • Backup files are checked file by file with hashes before they are restored.
  • Support email is kept in an account only we can access.

7. Cookies and trackers

The app has no cookies, advertising identifiers or tracking tools. The website sets no cookies; its sound, day/night and beret settings stay in your browser's storage.

8. Children

PASS LOOP has no account and we receive no personal information, so we don't ask for age. If a child under 14 uses outside AI services or links, a parent or guardian should check them together.
